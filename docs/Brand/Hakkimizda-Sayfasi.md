# Sınavika hakkında

**Sana göre sınav hazırlığı.**

Sınavika, sınava hazırlanan öğrencinin her gün açtığı çalışma yeri. Konu anlatımı, testler, denemeler, çalışma planı ve SinoAI tek uygulamada; web'de, iOS'ta ve Android'de. Amacımız iki cümleye sığıyor: ne çalışacağını bil, ilerlediğini gör.

## Neden varız

Sınava hazırlanırken en zor şey çoğu zaman bilgi bulmak değil, dağınıklıkla baş etmek. Bir yanda kitaplar, bir yanda videolar, bir yanda deneme sonuçları. Hangi konuya ne kadar zaman ayıracağını bilmeden çalışınca emeğin bir kısmı boşa gider. Netler yerinde sayar, motivasyon düşer.

Sınavika'yı bu yüzden kurduk. Misyonumuz: sınava hazırlanan her öğrencinin, nerede yaşarsa yaşasın, ne çalışacağını bilerek ve her gün biraz ilerleyerek hedefine yaklaşması.

Bunu tek bir döngüyle yapıyoruz: **Ölç, öğren, geliş.**
- **Ölç:** Deneme ve testlerde nerede olduğunu konu konu gör.
- **Öğren:** Eksik konunu kısa derslerle ve sorularla kapat.
- **Geliş:** Planına uy, ilerlemeni sayılarla takip et.

## Nasıl çalışıyoruz

### Önce senin verine bakarız

Sınavika "herkese aynı program" vermez. Deneme sonucundan eksik konun çıkar, eksik konundan bugünün görevi, görevden ilerlemen. Hedef sınavın, çözdüğün sorular ve çalıştığın süre bu zinciri besler. SinoAI de aynı bilgiyle konuşur.

### Öğretirken bir sıra izleriz

Her konu aynı yoldan ilerler:
- önce tanım;
- sonra kavramın ne anlama geldiği;
- formül, kavram anlaşıldıktan sonra;
- örnekler sorulardan önce.

Dersler kısa adımlara bölünür. Kaldığın yerden devam edersin, kısa bir arada bile bir adımı bitirebilirsin.

### İçeriği nasıl hazırlıyoruz

İçeriğin temeli resmî çerçeve:
- MEB öğretim programındaki kazanımlar;
- ÖSYM'nin soru tarzı ve konu dağılımı.

Bu kaynakları yalnız başvuru olarak kullanırız; resmî metinleri ve soruları kopyalamayız. Konu anlatımları, sorular ve çözümler Sınavika için hazırlanır. Soru şekilleri kendi çizim sistemimizle çizilir, böylece telefonda da webde de net ve aynı görünür.

### Kaliteyi nasıl denetliyoruz

- Bir soru yayına girmeden önce birkaç aday hazırlanır ve denetlenir. Kusurlu bulunan aday öğrenciye gösterilmez.
- Cevap anahtarı, çözüm adımları ve şekil birlikte kontrol edilir: soru neyi soruyorsa ekranda da o görünmeli.
- Ekranları ve soruları öğrencilerden gelen geri bildirimle düzeltiriz. Bir hata görürsen Destek'ten bize yaz; inceleyip düzeltiriz.

# About Sınavika

**Exam prep built around you.**

Sınavika is where students preparing for a big exam study every day. Lessons, quizzes, practice exams, a study plan and SinoAI live in one app, on the web, iOS and Android. Our goal fits in two lines: know what to study, and see yourself improve.

## Why we exist

The hardest part of exam prep usually isn't finding information. It's the clutter: a prep book here, a video there, a practice score somewhere else. When you don't know where your time should go, part of your effort is wasted. Scores stall and motivation drops.

That's why we built Sınavika. Our mission: every student preparing for an exam, wherever they live, moves closer to their goal by knowing what to study and getting a little better every day.

We do it with one loop: **Measure. Learn. Grow.**
- **Measure:** see where you stand, topic by topic, in quizzes and practice exams.
- **Learn:** close each gap with short lessons and practice questions.
- **Grow:** follow your plan and watch your progress in numbers.

## How we work

### We start from your data

Sınavika doesn't hand everyone the same schedule. Your practice exam shows your gaps, your gaps set today's tasks, and your tasks drive your progress. Your target exam, the questions you answer and the time you put in all feed that chain, and SinoAI works from the same picture.

### We teach in a set order

Every topic follows the same path:
- first the definition;
- then what the idea means;
- the formula once the idea makes sense;
- worked examples before practice questions.

Lessons are split into short steps, so you can pick up where you left off and finish a step even in a short break.

### How we build content

Every lesson and question is written for Sınavika and follows each exam's published format and content areas. We use official materials as a reference only and never copy them. Figures are drawn by our own system, so they look sharp and identical on your phone and on the web.

### How we check quality

- Before a question goes live, several drafts are written and reviewed. A draft with a flaw never reaches you.
- The answer key, the solution steps and the figure are checked together: what the question asks is what you see on screen.
- We fix screens and questions based on what students tell us. If you spot a mistake, write to Support and we'll look into it.

## Modules

**Study Plan.** Shows what to do today and which topics to review. Set your daily plan and track your hours.

**Lessons.** Teaches each topic in short micro-lessons. Learn step by step and pick up where you left off.

**Quizzes.** Tests you topic by topic, level by level. Every answer is explained instantly, and you move up when you're ready.

**Practice exams.** Timed exams that work like the real thing. You get a topic-by-topic breakdown and see your score change over time.

**Flashcards.** Brings back each card just before you'd forget it. Use short breaks and save the cards you want to see again.

**Smart Summary.** Snap a photo of a question you're stuck on. It solves it step by step and shows where you went wrong.

**SinoAI.** Sınavika's AI study assistant. It answers with your goal, your gaps and your past work in mind. It solves questions, sums up topics, and builds quizzes, practice exams and flashcards for you. It never pretends to be human, and it tells you when it isn't sure.

## What we value

**Accuracy first.** The question, the answer and the solution have to be right. If we're not sure, we don't publish it.

**You at the center.** Plans, suggestions and analysis come from your own results, not a one-size-fits-all schedule.

**Honesty.** No made-up numbers, no fake countdowns, no promise of guaranteed scores. What's paid is stated clearly, and canceling is as easy as subscribing.

**Support, not pressure.** After a rough practice exam we don't blame you. We show the topics that cost you the most and give you the next step.

**Privacy.** Your data is used only to help you, and we say so plainly. Details are in our Privacy Policy.

**Built for everyone.** Plain language, readable type, and screens that work on a slow connection. We talk about progress through effort, not talent.

## Frequently asked questions

**Is Sınavika free?**
Sınavika works on a subscription. In the mobile app you can try it free for 3 days through the App Store or Google Play; there's no free trial on the web. Plans and prices are listed on the Plans page. You can cancel anytime: on the web under Profile › Subscription, on mobile in your store settings.

**Which exams can I prepare for?**
The SAT, the ACT and AP. You pick your target exam when you sign up, and your lessons, quizzes and practice exams follow it.

**What is SinoAI, and is it always right?**
SinoAI is Sınavika's AI study assistant. It answers with your goal and your gaps in mind. Like any AI it can make mistakes, and it tells you when it isn't sure. For test dates and rules, it points you to the official source.

**Where do the questions come from?**
Every question and lesson is written for Sınavika and follows each exam's official format. We never copy official test questions, and every question is reviewed before it goes live.

**Which devices can I use?**
The web (sinavika.com), iPhone and Android. One account works everywhere, and your progress stays in sync.
